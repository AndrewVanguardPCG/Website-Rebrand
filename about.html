About the firm

Built by operators, for owners.

Vanguard Private Client Group is a private investment platform combining acquisitions, brokerage, construction, and management under one operator-led roof.

Our story

A firm built
around the asset.

Most platforms specialize. We integrate — because the best decisions on a real asset are made by people who own and operate them.

Founded in 2020 and headquartered at 797 State Street in New Haven, the firm has grown into a vertically integrated platform spanning private equity, commercial brokerage, construction, and management — with a portfolio of more than 400 multifamily units that we own and self-manage, and over $300M in brokered transaction volume.

We've earned CoStar Power Broker recognition, repositioned underperforming assets across Connecticut, and partnered with municipalities, capital partners, and operators on adaptive reuse and ground-up development. The throughline is alignment: we put our own capital and reputation on every deal we touch.
