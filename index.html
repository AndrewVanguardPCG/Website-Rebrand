Who we are

An operator,
first.

We don't sit on the sidelines. We own, manage, develop, and broker — which means every deal we touch is underwritten with real-world operating assumptions.

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in New Haven, Vanguard Private Client Group is a vertically integrated platform across private equity, commercial brokerage, construction, and management. Our portfolio includes more than 400 multifamily units that we own and self-manage, and over $300M in brokered transaction volume — earning CoStar Power Broker recognition along the way.

What sets us apart is alignment. We invest alongside partners, manage what we own, and structure transactions — including seller financing and phased exits — designed for certainty of close and downside protection.
